What happened

Between 9 and 11 September 2026, an unauthorized actor gained access to an internal, non-customer-facing environment connected to a restricted, read-only replica containing a limited subset of production data. As part of our response, we reset every Crypgo user's password and invalidated all active sessions across all accounts, as a precaution — not because we found evidence tied specifically to yours.

Based on our completed investigation, we found no evidence that your account was among those for which integration credentials or account-related information were accessed during this incident. We're contacting affected customers directly with more details relevant to their accounts.

Why we reset every password anyway

Crypgo passwords are always stored hashed and salted, never in plain text, so even where account data was involved in this incident, passwords were not exposed in a usable form. We reset every account's password regardless, simply because we'd rather take that step across the board than assume any one account is definitely fine.
